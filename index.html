ONGOING RESEARCH · URBAN SYSTEMS

Navigation, Urban Traffic & Carbon Emissions

An ongoing simulation study examining how navigation adoption, routing choices, and traffic demand interact to affect congestion and vehicle emissions on a real urban road network.

  • Built and cleaned a directed road network with OSMnx and constructed route-planning experiments using 2,000 random origin–destination pairs.
  • Converted the network into SUMO-compatible form and controlled microscopic traffic simulation through TraCI.
  • Implemented second-by-second, vehicle-level CO₂ logging and designed comparative scenarios across traffic loads, routing strategies, and navigation-adoption rates.
  • Investigating nonlinear system-level effects rather than assuming that individually shorter routes always reduce aggregate emissions.

Machine Learning · Team course project

Urban Taxi Supply–Demand Forecasting & Dispatch

96

A closed-loop pipeline using January–June 2016 NYC yellow-taxi trips, designed to forecast region-hour shortage pressure from raw coordinates and translate predictions into dispatch decisions.

From raw trajectories to operational decisionsTwo-stage spatial clustering → OD network construction → spatio-temporal features → XGBoost risk classification → multi-objective linear programming.
  • Cleaned 1,458,644 trip records to 1,440,999 valid trips using temporal, spatial, duration, and speed rules.
  • Identified 235 DBSCAN micro-hotspots and aggregated them into 25 weighted K-Means macro-regions.
  • Built approximately 70 features, including temporal cycles, lags, PageRank, flow balance, eigenvector centrality, and flow entropy.
  • Reached 76.31% recall for the severe-shortage class on a chronological test set; macro F1 was 0.4759.
  • Used predicted probabilities in a continuous dispatch model that eliminated identified shortfalls in the reported test scenario.

Econometrics · Individual laboratory series

UGC Usefulness & Econometric Diagnostics

84

A seven-part empirical laboratory sequence centered on online-platform content, supplemented by a time-series exercise. The work moved beyond coefficient reporting to test model assumptions and compare corrective estimators.

18,101 platform observationsModeled engagement usefulness using creator popularity, text length, sentiment intensity, readability, photos, emojis, verification, tags, and time controls.
  • Built simple and multiple OLS specifications, log transforms, dummy variables, interaction effects, and marginal-effect visualizations in Stata.
  • Used LOWESS, polynomial terms, AIC/BIC, RESET, stepwise selection, Lasso, correlation diagnostics, and VIF to examine functional form and specification.
  • Diagnosed heteroskedasticity with residual plots, Goldfeld–Quandt and White tests; compared OLS, WLS, and heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors.
  • Studied serial correlation with Durbin–Watson and Breusch–Godfrey tests, then compared Cochrane–Orcutt-style transformation, Prais–Winsten, and Newey–West HAC inference.
  • Explored endogeneity and instrument relevance using first-stage diagnostics, IV/2SLS, and an endogeneity test.
