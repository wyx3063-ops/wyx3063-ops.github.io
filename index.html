02 · Research

Selected research and applied projects.

ONGOING RESEARCH · 01

Urban Traffic Simulation & Carbon Emission Analysis

Investigating how navigation adoption, route choice, and traffic demand interact to shape congestion and system-level vehicle emissions on a real urban road network.

  • Built and cleaned a directed road network and generated 2,000 random origin–destination pairs.
  • Converted OSMnx networks for SUMO and configured microscopic simulations through TraCI.
  • Implemented second-by-second, vehicle-level CO₂ logging and comparative scenario analysis.
  • Examining nonlinear relationships between navigation adoption and aggregate emissions.

PROJECT · 02

Store-Level Sales Forecasting & Transfer Learning

A multi-source forecasting workflow designed to support replenishment and store-level operational decisions.

  • Integrated sales, weather, holiday, business-district, and customer features.
  • Built and tuned store-specific XGBoost models evaluated with MAPE.
  • Combined hierarchical clustering and Dynamic Time Warping to identify similar stores and transfer model parameters to new or low-volume stores.
XGBoostDTWHierarchical Clustering
PROJECT · 03

Consumer Segmentation & Repurchase Prediction

An empirical study of consumer behavior and repurchase mechanisms in an urban commercial district.

  • Designed the survey workflow and performed reliability, validity, and factor analysis.
  • Applied K-Means for segmentation and decision trees and random forests for churn warning and repurchase prediction.
  • Used IPA analysis to translate findings into pricing, queue, and product recommendations.
Factor AnalysisK-MeansRandom Forest
ONGOING STUDY · 04

Generative AI in Higher Education

Mixed-method research examining how students use generative AI and how it may influence learning processes and academic outcomes.

  • Combined questionnaire and interview evidence to study adoption and learning behavior.
  • Applied path analysis and mediation analysis to investigate potential mechanisms.
  • Focused on careful empirical interpretation rather than treating association as causation.
